Ep. 2 – A Pirate Must Plunder

Oct 21st, 2026

Recap

The morning after the Alloy Hold heist, the party woke at Ares Manor outside Blackstone to a breakfast that was a feast in itself. Kira had advised a week of lying low while the search parties died down, and the party weighed that against its open business: Kira’s package for Purac, the cure for Hamilton’s eye in Edendrawn, and reports that the Guardians of Tradition were planning an attack on Blackstone at some unknown time. Seph found it very funny that the real pregnant angel she had impersonated would now be a wanted woman. Bwomsamdi, who had kept out of the heist itself, tried to find any trace of Elizabeth Velmori in Blackstone and found none.

Kira arrived nursing a hangover and pouring more champagne. She described Harrentoon, the realm of the giants: a small world hidden in a demiplane, practically another continent, with its own laws, cultures and factions. The way there was by small sailboat down the river to Kreglog and then a larger ship across the sea to Costomore, about six days in all. When D’ith worried that the trip would delay her package, Kira said she could always find another mule, so the party left the package with her for someone else to deliver.

She offered a different job instead. The giants owe her a couple of hundred thousand platinum for projects she financed, to be settled with an artefact, and collecting it would save her a boat ride. Their ruler, Tondon, is a storm giant. Giant society ranks people mainly by strength and fighting ability, with some weight on brains; the leaders tend to be level-headed and the lower rungs are where it gets dangerous. A letter would do no good, but Kira taught them a phrase in Giant that she uses fondly with the leader, which should earn them safe passage as long as they respect giant customs. Bwomsamdi, who speaks Giant and counts himself a distant descendant of giants, will translate. He thanked Kira with a bottle of vintage champagne from his Bag of Holding, which he admitted he had probably found in Ares Manor’s own cellar.

Seph chose to stay with the party for now. The Staff of the Demon Queen, she said, matters only as a way to the crown beneath the Amethyst Academy Arcana, which her sister stopped her from taking last year. Magic down there is so strong it warps other magic, and her sister had foresight cast on her there and now hears the screams of a thousand versions of herself. The crown is made from the final embers of the archdevils’ souls, from when they died in the Divinities Collapse, and wearing it grants the power of the Hells. Seph wants it before a cult manages to resummon the dead ruler of the Hells. She promised to stay out of the material plane once she has her throne, agreed to break the party out of jail if it ever came to that, and said yes when Bwomsamdi asked to be one of her Hell’s Angels.

Over breakfast the party sorted its gear. Hamilton admitted his eye now talks to him, saying things like “eat, feed me, I’m hungry”. D’ith retired the Crown of Telepathy in favour of the Staff of Twinning. Hamilton drew the Holy Avenger, whose longsword blade extends from the hilt in white flame. Chaddicus confirmed the Blade of Kas holds Call Lightning, Divine Word and Finger of Death, and learned that some of its powers stay locked until Vecna either regains his full power or dies. Hamilton told Bwomsamdi how Trish Massfor stole the Orb of Dragonkind. Bwomsamdi then had a vision: Billy told him to win the giants’ favour and make them allies, and Hamilton suggested asking them to help defend Blackstone.

The party sold its spare gear, including a rapier, two pairs of bracers, the Cowl of Comprehend Languages, the Crown of Telepathy, ivory tusks, incense, gems, a pair of sandals and a quarterstaff of health potions, for about 22,000 gold, split 5,500 each. Bwomsamdi gave Hamilton an Amulet of Psychic Resistance and Chaddicus took Hamilton’s stun baton. Chaddicus then paid Bwomsamdi 7,000 gold for the belt Bwomsamdi had lent him the money for, which clears all his debts.

With far too much gold to carry, Bwomsamdi asked Kira about investments. She holds a contract to rebuild Khatdun, still a wreck since the battle there, and needs directors for a small shipping company to fund the work. Bwomsamdi and Hamilton put in 10,000 gold each and became three-way partners with Kira, who will start work in their absence and have the contracts ready to sign when they return. The partners named the company Hamsamdi Shipping, a blend of their two names, after the table rejected Ice Road Truckers and Shippity-doo-dah. Kira told them their boat would wait at the Riverside Dock at the second bell, under a Captain Viceroy.

Before leaving, Hamilton visited Kathy’s Boutique, a high-end clothing shop, and bought a deep forest-green tricorn hat with a satin finish, a leather rim and a peacock feather, then a matching forest-green trench coat lined in lilac, warm and water resistant. He overpaid for both. The party spent the visit insisting the boutique was a pirate shop until the clerk asked them to leave. D’ith picked up rations on the way to the river.

Captain Viceroy, a tall human with shoulder-length ginger hair and a stubbly beard, greeted them as the wanted criminals and squeezed them onto a cramped sailboat for most of a day downriver. They reached Kreglog in the early morning: a small walled city taken up mostly by its docks and harbour. Burn marks covered the waterfront from a fire about a year ago, when people blew up a ship carrying illegal purple crystals. No one was ever arrested. Hamilton blamed followers of Rarey, and Viceroy warned him that a lot of pirates follow Rarey too.

While they waited, Bwomsamdi found a shaken fisherman being comforted by his friends. On a pitch-black night something had rocked and struck his ship, a huge bulbous shape with what looked like a tentacle, and swallowed his crew whole. He wept at the thought that it was a kraken. The other fishermen said such stories had never been proven and the crossing to Costomore had always been safe, but Bwomsamdi judged that the man was not lying.

Viceroy returned with a much larger sailboat with a lower deck and hammocks, promising five days to Costomore in exchange for some help with the rigging and mopping. D’ith worked the ropes with Mage Hand and Hamilton took the crow’s nest. On the third day Hamilton spotted black flags closing fast. Bwomsamdi called on Billy through divine intervention and raised a whirlpool under the pirate ship. Chaddicus leapt across, shouted “Who wants to join my crew?” and struck a pirate with Finger of Death from the Blade of Kas. D’ith flew over on his sorcerous wings and used the same spell on the two pirates working the cannons, raising them as zombies under his command. Divine Word from the blade drove the surviving boarders mad; one turned his own pistol on himself and others were left blind and deaf on the boarding ropes.

Then a wave struck and a massive shadow slid beneath both ships. Captain Viceroy told everyone, “Gentlemen, I think we should get a move on.” Hamilton shouted for everyone to get back aboard, but Chaddicus crashed down through the pirate ship’s deck into the hold shouting “A pirate must plunder!”, and Hamilton followed him. Their own crew, unaware of any of this, fired two cannonballs through the hold, which both of them dodged.

The looting turned up a tiny pocket watch in a velvet case far too big for it, with no magic that Bwomsamdi could detect; a gold headdress engraved with reptiles, which reminded Chaddicus of the lizardfolk where Hamilton got his eye; a map from the captain’s quarters with an X just off the coast of Costomore; jewellery and gold; and a twisted, hollowed ram’s horn bored with holes like a flute, carved with a script close to Dwarvish but not quite.

Something seized the pirate ship and began dragging it down as water rose around the looters’ ankles. D’ith burned through the last rope and told Viceroy to get moving, and the ship pulled away with Chaddicus and Hamilton still aboard the pirate vessel, Hamilton realising too late that he cannot fly. Bwomsamdi swapped back to the party’s ship through his echo. The session ended as a huge amethyst-coloured whale breached between the ships, its fins wrapped in chains and anchors and glowing with an ethereal light, and crashed down onto the pirate ship with a scream like whale song mixed with a ship’s horn, filling the air with the stink of rotten fish.

The party left Ares Manor after one night instead of lying low for a week. Kira swapped her package job for a new one: collect the artefact the giants of Harrentoon owe her, across the sea in Costomore. Seph revealed she is after a crown beneath the Amethyst Academy Arcana that grants the power of the Hells. The party sold its spare gear for about 22,000 gold, and Bwomsamdi and Hamilton invested 10,000 each in Kira’s shipping venture to rebuild Khatdun, named Hamsamdi Shipping.

Captain Viceroy took them downriver to Kreglog, where a fisherman claimed a kraken swallowed his crew. Three days into the crossing, pirates attacked. The party won the fight and looted the pirate ship, finding a map marked just off Costomore and three odd objects, but the crew cut the ship loose with Chaddicus and Hamilton still aboard just as an amethyst whale in chains crashed down on it.

Last we left off, the bastards had robbed the Alloy Hold and gone to ground in a manor full of champagne and favours. The north would be crawling with search parties for a week. Blackstone waited on an attack that might come any day. And across the sea, a court of giants owed a fortune to a woman who does not forget her debts.

So they did not lie low. They took a hangover’s blessing, a phrase in Giant and a boat down the river. They gave their gold to a shipping company and their dignity to a boutique. In a scorched harbour town a fisherman wept about the thing that ate his crew, and nobody quite believed him. Three days out, the black flags came, and steel and dark magic answered them.

But the sea was listening. As two of their own stood knee-deep in plunder on a sinking ship, the rope that tied them to safety burned away, and from the deep rose something amethyst and vast, wrapped in the chains of every ship it had ever taken.

Ordered to lie low for a week, the party lasted one breakfast before booking a cruise to go and shake down some storm giants. Kira didn’t mind: she swapped her urgent package for a debt collection job, which is a promotion if you don’t think about the giants. Bwomsamdi then thanked her with a bottle of champagne he almost certainly stole from her own cellar.

Flush with 22,000 gold from selling everything not nailed down, two of the party founded a shipping company, and Hamilton spent the rest becoming the best-dressed privateer in a shop that was very clear it is not a pirate shop. A fisherman told them a sea monster ate his crew. They nodded politely and got on a boat.

When pirates attacked, the party won so hard that two of them jumped into the enemy ship to loot it, mid-battle, while their own crew shot cannonballs through the room they were standing in. Then D’ith cut the rope, the ship sailed off without them, and a glowing whale wearing anchors as jewellery arrived to make its point. Tune in next time.

Kira waved off her package with ease And sent them to giants overseas. A debt to collect, Some respect to project, And a phrase that should help them say please.

Bwomsamdi had gold to invest, So Hamilton chipped in the rest. For Khatdun they’ll ship Under Hamsamdi’s grip, And the hat that he bought is the best.

Three days out, black flags hove in sight, And the whirlpool of Billy took flight. The Blade spoke its Word, D’ith’s zombies were stirred, And the pirates went mad from the fright.

Chaddicus crashed through to plunder, And Hamilton followed him under. The rope was cut free, And up from the sea Rose a whale with a scream like thunder.
