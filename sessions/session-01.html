Ep. 1 – The Alloy Hold Heist

Oct 18th, 2026

Recap

Before this session the party had freed Director Asimov from Elizabeth Velmori’s mind control, destroyed her lab and sent Asimov back to Sunhome to clear his name. They then crossed the desert above ground to the Alloy Hold, a huge and heavily guarded fortress, to find Commander Neptune Nikos and to get hold of some magical items. Seph, the party’s ally who wants to rule the Hells, arrived disguised as a pregnant angel. Chaddicus disguised himself as a pregnant woman to match.

Neptune, a young man with royal blue hair who walks on crutches since the battle at Katdoon, called the party “the bastards” and was nearly given away when Hamilton asked how he knew Seph. A deception check and a story about a knock to the head covered it. D'ith briefed Neptune on Velmori’s lab, her clones of the King and the nobility, and the files the party had seized. After a nat 1 and then two 24s on persuasion, Neptune signed a note permitting an investigation under supervision, with nothing to be touched or removed.

Neptune took Seph to the command room and Seph passed D'ith an Ascending Stone, a sending stone. The party then used the lift operator Clayson to reach the vault floors 8 to 10, about 500 feet down, where an anti-magic field begins below floor 8. The DM capped theft at five items. With D'ith talking, Hamilton lifting keys and rune stones, and Chaddicus on distraction, the party stripped the guards to their underwear on a “clones found upstairs” ruse and walked off with the Staff of the Demon Queen (which burned Hamilton on touch), the Blade of Kas, the Staff of Twinning, the Efreeti Chain and the Holy Avenger, which turned out to be only a hilt. A goblin thief called Trish Massfor took the Orb of Dragonkind from under their noses.

In the command room Seph used the Staff to open a fiery vortex as guards burst in, and the party landed in a forest near Blackstone. Ritual identify revealed the items. Chaddicus took the Efreeti Chain and attuned to the Blade of Kas, whose sentient spirit woke with “And who the fuck are you now?” and wants revenge on Lucille. At Ares Manor, Seph’s aunt Kira offered shelter for about a week and asked the party to carry a sealed package south to Purac. The session ended on a long rest, and the DM called it the best series of rolls the group had ever made.

The party talked their way into the Alloy Hold, robbed its vault of five artefacts without killing anyone, escaped through a fiery vortex to Blackstone and now owe Seph’s aunt a delivery.

Beneath the desert, a fortress kept its treasures 500 feet down and its secrets closer still. The bastards walked in on borrowed lies and a pregnant disguise, left the guards in their underwear, and took a sword that remembers its killer. Fire opened the floor of the world. They fell out the other side into a forest, richer, hunted and a little burned.

The party’s plan to avoid violence went flawlessly, mostly because Neptune Nikos believed a signed note, a pregnant barbarian and a briefcase full of files. Every guard was robbed, one goblin robbed the robbers, and Hamilton still touched the cursed staff. The best rolls the group has ever made, spent on a burglary.

Through the Hold in a pregnant disguise, The bastards told masterful lies. The guards stripped to pants, Seph made a fire dance, And they left with a five-item prize.
